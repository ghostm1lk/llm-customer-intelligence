# ATM Cash Not Dispensed (Nova Bank)

If an ATM debits the account but does not dispense cash (or dispenses less than requested):

- The customer should report it immediately with the ATM location, date, time and amount.
- For Nova Bank ATMs, the ATM is balanced within 2 business days and the amount is returned automatically if a surplus is found.
- For other banks' ATMs (JoNet network), the investigation takes up to 7 business days.
- The customer receives an SMS once the amount is credited back.
- If the ATM retained the card, see the policy on lost, stolen and blocked cards.
