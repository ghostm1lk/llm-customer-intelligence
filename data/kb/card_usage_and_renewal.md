# Card Usage, Limits and Renewal (Nova Bank)

- Cards are renewed automatically. The new card is sent to the registered branch 30 days before expiry and the customer is notified by SMS.
- Default daily limits: 1,000 JOD ATM withdrawal, 3,000 JOD purchases. Limits can be raised up to 3,000 JOD (ATM) in the app under "Card limits", effective immediately.
- International usage is disabled by default. Customers must enable "Use abroad" in the app before travelling. It can be enabled per country and per date range.
- Online (e-commerce) usage can be switched on or off in the app.
