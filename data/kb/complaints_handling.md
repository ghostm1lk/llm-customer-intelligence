# Complaints Handling (Nova Bank)

- Every complaint receives a reference number and an acknowledgement within 1 business day.
- Standard complaints are resolved within 5 business days.
- Repeated complaints (the same issue raised 2 or more times) and complaints mentioning regulators or legal action are escalated automatically to a senior complaints officer and must be resolved within 2 business days.
- If the customer is not satisfied with the final answer, they can escalate to the Central Bank of Jordan's financial consumer protection department.
- Complaints about staff behaviour are forwarded to the branch manager and reviewed within 3 business days.
