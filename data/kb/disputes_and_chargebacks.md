# Card Disputes and Chargebacks (Nova Bank)

Customers can dispute a card transaction when a merchant does not deliver, keeps charging after a cancellation, or fails to process a promised refund.

- Disputes must be raised within 60 days of the transaction date.
- The customer must first contact the merchant; if the merchant does not resolve it within 10 days, the bank opens a chargeback.
- Required evidence: cancellation confirmation, return receipt, or merchant correspondence.
- Chargebacks take up to 45 days. A temporary credit may be given after 10 business days for amounts under 500 JOD.
- Merchant refunds after a product return usually take 5 to 14 business days to appear on the account.
- Recurring subscription charges after cancellation are handled as disputes, not as fraud.
