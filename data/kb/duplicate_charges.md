# Duplicate Charges Policy (Nova Bank)

A duplicate charge is when the same merchant, amount and date appear more than once on a customer's account.

- Many "duplicates" are temporary authorization holds. Holds usually disappear by themselves within 3 business days.
- If both charges are still posted after 3 business days, the customer can file a duplicate charge claim through the app, by phone, or at any branch.
- The Billing Department must verify the transaction logs within 24 hours of receiving the claim.
- If the duplicate is confirmed, the extra amount is refunded to the account within 5 business days.
- Duplicate loan installment deductions follow the same process and are refunded within 5 business days.
- The customer should provide: transaction date, amount, and merchant name.
