# Fees and Charges (Nova Bank)

- Withdrawal from Nova Bank ATMs: free.
- Withdrawal from other local banks' ATMs: 0.500 JOD per withdrawal.
- Withdrawal from ATMs abroad: 5 JOD plus 1.5% of the amount.
- Foreign currency card transactions: 2.5% currency conversion fee. Merchants abroad may offer to charge in JOD (dynamic currency conversion); their rate is often worse than the bank's.
- Credit card annual fee: 25 JOD (Classic), 60 JOD (Gold). The annual fee is waived if the customer spends more than 2,000 JOD in the card year. Fee refunds outside this rule are not offered, except when the card was cancelled within 30 days of the fee being charged.
- Late payment fee on credit cards: 10 JOD. If the payment was made on or before the due date but posted late because of a bank delay, the fee is reversed on request.
- Overdraft fee: 3 JOD per occurrence. Customers with overdraft protection are not charged this fee; if charged, it is refunded.
- Savings account minimum balance: 100 JOD. Below this, a monthly fee of 1 JOD applies.
