# Fraud and Unauthorized Transactions (Nova Bank)

An unauthorized transaction is any transaction the customer did not make or approve.

- The customer should report it immediately through the 24/7 fraud line (06-500-0000) or the app ("Report fraud").
- The Fraud Team blocks the card immediately and opens an investigation.
- If the customer reports within 2 days, they are not liable for any unauthorized amount. After 2 days, liability is capped at 50 JOD, unless the customer shared their PIN or OTP.
- Investigations are completed within 10 business days; confirmed fraud amounts are refunded in full.
- Small unknown charges (e.g. 1 JOD) are often "test charges" used by fraudsters before a larger purchase. They must be treated as fraud.

Phishing:
- Nova Bank never asks for a PIN, full card number, password or OTP by phone, SMS or email, and never sends login links by SMS or email.
- If a customer entered card details on a suspicious link, the card must be blocked immediately and a new card issued free of charge.
- If a customer received a suspicious call or message but shared nothing, they should report it so the Fraud Team can warn other customers; no action on the account is needed.
