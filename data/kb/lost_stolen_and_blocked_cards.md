# Lost, Stolen and Blocked Cards (Nova Bank)

- Lost or stolen cards must be blocked immediately via the app ("Freeze card") or the 24/7 line (06-500-0000). A replacement card is issued within 5 business days for a fee of 5 JOD (free if stolen and a police report is provided).
- Cards retained by a Nova Bank ATM are kept at the ATM's branch for 7 days and can be collected with a valid ID. Cards retained by another bank's ATM are destroyed and replaced free of charge.
- After 3 wrong PIN attempts the card is blocked for security. It can be unblocked in the app under "Reset PIN" or at any branch. Unblocking is immediate.
- Declined card payments despite sufficient balance are usually caused by: daily limit reached, card not enabled for online or international use, or an expired card.
