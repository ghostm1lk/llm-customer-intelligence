# Online and Mobile Banking Access (Nova Bank)

- After 5 wrong password attempts, online banking is locked for 30 minutes and unlocks automatically. After a second lock on the same day, the customer must reset the password.
- Password reset: "Forgot password" in the app, verified by OTP sent to the registered mobile number.
- OTP codes are only sent to the registered mobile number. If the customer changed their phone number, they must update it at a branch or ATM (with card and PIN) before OTPs can be received. This takes effect within 1 hour.
- If the customer still cannot log in, Account Services can reset access after identity verification by phone.
