# Personal and Car Loans (Nova Bank)

- Personal loan interest rate: from 7.5% (fixed, annual) for salaried customers who transfer their salary to Nova Bank, 9% otherwise. Maximum term: 8 years.
- Car loan interest rate: from 6.9% (fixed, annual). Maximum term: 7 years.
- Early repayment: allowed at any time. Early settlement fee is 1% of the remaining principal, waived if the loan is older than 3 years.
- Loan applications are decided within 5 business days of receiving complete documents.
- Payment deferral: customers facing hardship (e.g. job loss) can request a deferral of up to 3 installments per year. Interest continues to accrue during the deferral. Requests must be made at least 5 days before the installment date.
