# Transfers and Branch Information (Nova Bank)

International transfers (SWIFT):
- Fee: 10 JOD plus correspondent bank charges (usually 5 to 15 USD).
- Timing: 1 to 3 business days to Europe, 2 to 5 business days to other regions.
- If a transfer has not arrived after 5 business days, the customer can request a SWIFT trace (free). Results within 3 business days.

Local transfers (CliQ): instant, free up to 5,000 JOD per day.

Branches:
- Opening hours: Sunday to Thursday 8:30 to 15:00, Saturday 9:00 to 13:00 (selected branches). Closed Fridays.
- Branches in Amman, Zarqa, Irbid, Aqaba and Salt. The branch locator is in the app.
